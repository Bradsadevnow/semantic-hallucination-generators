Crystal units have no monetary value, which is exactly how they become structurally necessary.

Unit one

Crystal Shards

Small, bright, and psychologically efficient. The ideal entry point for users who would like to spend a little without admitting to themselves that the platform has begun valuing them in fragments.

  • Session boosts and low-tier unlocks
  • Minor acceleration for premium surfaces
  • Beautiful enough to imply scarcity
Unit two

Crystal Rocks

The middle denomination for users who want their transactions to feel technical. Rocks are how a simple in-app purchase becomes developer infrastructure with a straight face.

  • Prosumer budgeting for `Gloss` calls
  • Bulk access posture without full enterprise shame
  • Perfect for pricing things that should not need a second currency
Unit three

Crystal Bricks

Brick format is for whale accounts, enterprise allocations, and anyone who mistakes industrial packaging for legitimacy. A crystal brick is less a currency unit than a personality test.

  • Enterprise reserve provisioning
  • Volume-based access theater
  • Large enough to feel administratively important
What crystal can buy

A broad range of things that should have remained simple.

The crystal layer is intentionally overgeneralized. It can denominate entertainment loops, API usage, premium routing, labor marketplace actions, and whatever else the platform decides would benefit from value obfuscation.

  • Gloss access and “premium reasoning” allocation
  • MONETIZATION THEATER boosts, currencies, and retention-shaped upgrades
  • Marketplace actions for future labor or agent surfaces
  • Priority routing, unlocks, and any other scam geometry the suite discovers later
Official notice

Crystal units are non-redeemable, non-refundable, non-transferable, and explicitly non-monetary. Continued reliance on them does not alter this status.

By understanding these terms you acknowledge that the platform may continue making them feel essential.
